Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Bits & Gates (bitsandgates.com) is an educational tool for designing and simulating logic circuits and exploring a small 8-bit computer. This policy explains what information it handles and why. In short: we keep only what's needed to save your circuits, and we don't use advertising or tracking.

Using it without signing in

You can use the circuit library, the CPU Lab, and the Commodore-style terminal without an account. We don't ask for or store any personal information when you do.

Signing in

Sign-in is provided by Auth0 (part of Okta). When you sign in with Google or another provider, Auth0 receives your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider. Auth0 stores this profile in its UK region, and uses it to identify your account. Your password is never shared with Bits & Gates.

The Bits & Gates server itself stores only an account identifier issued by Auth0. It uses that identifier to link the circuits you save to your account. Your name and picture are shown in the app, but our server does not store them.

Your circuits

Circuits you save are stored on our server, linked to your account identifier. They are private to you: other users cannot see them. You can delete any of your circuits at any time from within the app.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. To keep you signed in, your browser stores sign-in tokens in its local storage. Auth0 also sets cookies on its own domain to manage your sign-in session. Signing out removes the tokens from your browser.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on a server operated by Hetzner Online in Finland (EU). The server keeps short, rotating technical error logs to help fix problems. We do not record your browsing activity.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. The only third parties involved are Auth0, which provides sign-in, the provider you choose to sign in with (such as Google), and Hetzner, which hosts the server.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information held about you at any time. To delete your account and all of your saved circuits, email us from the address you signed in with. You can also withdraw access at any time through your Google account's security settings.

Children

Bits & Gates is suitable for learners of all ages, but accounts are intended for people aged 13 and over. Younger learners can use everything without signing in; only saving circuits needs an account.
