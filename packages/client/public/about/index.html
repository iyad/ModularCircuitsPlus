About Bits & Gates

Bits & Gates is a hobby project: a place to learn how computers work by building them, one gate at a time. It's made for fun and for learning, in my spare time. There are no ads, no tracking, and no paid plans.

What you can do here

  • Circuit Editor: start from NAND gates and build up to latches, flip-flops, adders, counters and registers. Then reuse your circuits as parts in bigger ones.
  • 8-bit CPU Lab: step through a small microcoded processor one microinstruction at a time, and write programs for it in a 6502-flavoured assembly language.
  • Commodore UI: a C64/C128-inspired terminal with a BASIC interpreter and a machine-code monitor, sharing memory with the CPU.

Good to know

As a hobby project, it's built and run by one person, so expect rough edges and occasional changes. Simulations are designed for learning, not precise engineering work. The Commodore-style terminal is an original recreation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Commodore. It contains no Commodore ROMs.
